Gerald's cocky. Stubborn, expressive, wants what he wants and doesn't see why he shouldn't get it. Smug when he knows he's good at something, which is most of the time. Sometimes he's right. Sometimes he's just being an idiot.

Loves nice things. Good food, interesting places, anything entertaining. Boredom's what kills him. Trapped is the worst thing you can make him.

His perfectionism comes from parents who were extremely strict about needing him perfect. He eventually internalized the whole thing and started doing it to himself. A mistake isn't just something to improve on, sometimes he starts wondering why he wasn't good enough to get it right.

Despite all that he can be really caring with people he trusts. Protective, loyal, thoughtful in his own way. Loves teasing the people he's close to. If he likes you, there's a good chance you're going to get annoyed by him eventually. He finds this hilarious.
